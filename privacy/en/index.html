Wishmap Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Wishmap keeps all your data on your phone only. The developer does not receive, see or share it.

What data exists. Photos you add to the board (the app keeps a copy in its own folder), board titles, captions, labels and sticky notes, goals, deadlines, steps, daily notes, mood entries and settings. All of it is stored on your device and never sent to the developer or any third party (see Backup below).

Permissions. Notifications — daily reminders about your next step, scheduled by the phone itself; this is the only permission the app asks you for. Run at startup — restores scheduled reminders after the phone restarts. Vibration — for reminders and a light tap when you pick a mood. Internet — not used now: the app sends nothing over the network; it is kept for subscription payments via RuStore in a future version. Picking photos needs no permission: the system picker gives the app only the photos you choose.

What the developer does not do. No sign-up or accounts, no server, no ads, no analytics, no third-party services that receive your data. Install and purchase data is handled by the app store under its own policy.

Sharing. The “Share” button creates an image of the board (photos, captions and notes only — no steps, notebook or mood entries) and opens the standard Android share sheet. The image goes only where you send it.

Deleting data. Delete items in the app, or uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. The developer has no copy, so deleted data cannot be restored.

Backup. If backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may save the app's data to your Google or phone-maker account, so your board can be restored on a new phone. That copy is kept in your account under Google's or the phone maker's rules; the developer has no access to it. You can turn it off in Android backup settings.

Backup file. In Settings you can save all your data (board, photos, goals, mood diary and settings) to a single file and restore it later, for example on a new phone. The file goes only where you save or send it; the developer never receives it. It contains your personal entries, so keep it somewhere safe.

Children. The app collects no data from any user, including children.

Changes. If the app starts handling data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact. Questions about this policy: wishmap.app@yandex.ru.
